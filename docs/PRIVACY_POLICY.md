## In short

WorkEarn works entirely on your device. It has no account system and no server. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or
share any personal data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What the app stores

When you use the app it stores the following information **only in a private database on your device**:

- employers you add (name, colour, notes) and their pay rules (wage, bonuses, rounding),
- your shifts (start and end time, pauses and breaks, tips, extra earnings, notes) and the earnings calculated from them,
- your settings (theme, language, currency, public-holiday country, display preferences),
- a list of public holidays (generated on the device) and any custom holidays you add.

This information is created by you, stays on your device, and is not accessible to other apps. The app does not read your contacts,
location, photos, files, microphone, camera or any other personal data, and does not request any Android permission.

## Network access

The app does not request internet access and cannot send data over the network.

## Exporting your data

You can export your shifts as a CSV file. The file is created on your device and handed to the app **you** choose in the Android share
sheet (for example your email app or a cloud drive). From that moment that app's own privacy policy applies. WorkEarn itself never uploads
the file.

## Backups

Automatic Android backups are disabled for this app, so your data is not copied to Google Drive or another cloud by Android's backup
service. As a result it is also not restored automatically when you set up a new phone; use the CSV export if you want to keep a copy.

## Third parties

WorkEarn does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party tracking components.

## Children

WorkEarn is a general-purpose work and earnings tracker and is not directed at children.

## Your control

You can edit or delete any shift or employer at any time, and you can delete **all** data in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the
app also removes its data from the device.

## Your rights

Because the developer does not receive or process your data, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct or erase.
If you believe otherwise, contact the address above.
